Do kids really have an advantage when it comes to learning new things? Is there a point where we’re to old to learn something new? Do older adults learn slower than young kids, if they can learn new things at all?

Serious scientific research can resolve these questions. One factor that I know impacts learning is fear of failure. Especially public failure. Somehow society is a lot more forgiving to kids when they fail than to adults in a similar situation. Or perhaps older adults hold themselves to a higher standard. They expect to show up and be great at something.

To test this, I signed up for snowboarding lessons along with my newphew and niece with the same instructor at the same place, in the same class. To my surprise, the adults overall did better than kids during this class.

Onto the next experiment!
