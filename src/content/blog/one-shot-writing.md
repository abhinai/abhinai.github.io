There are two schools of thought when writing blog posts. First one dictates that you compose a blog post then keep revising it until it becomes perfect. The second one recommends writing as if you’re talking. The blog posts are imperfect but flow very naturally.

I prefer the second one. I call it one shot writing. All my blog posts are written in less than 10 minutes from start to finish and talk about exactly one topic.

I tend to chase perfection in everything in life. So why should I prefer the imperfect writing style of one-shot writing? Because from experience I’ve noticed that when I write without stopping to think, I use slightly different muscles in the brain and the end result is somehow very natural. The arguments are a bit clearer and cleaner. There is little overthinking involved.

This blog post was written in about 4 mins. :)
