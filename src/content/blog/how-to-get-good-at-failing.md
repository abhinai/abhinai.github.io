*This post builds on my previous post:* [*Failing is important.*](/writing/failing-is-important/)

To succeed at anything, we need to fail first. To be successful in life, we need to fail a lot. Failing sucks. Failing a lot will suck a lot unless we get good at failing.

So how do we get good at failing? HINT: Observe children.

Children fall down and fail and fall down again and fail again. Yet, they don’t seem to mind. In fact they actually enjoy the process. It’s called “Playing”.

Everyone thinks that children are good at learning because their brains are malleable. I think it’s also because they don’t mind failing and sucking at things. In fact everyone expects them to fail all the time. When they’re very young they can barely speak full sentences and no-one cares. They can barely walk and no one cares. Later they can suck at playing a piano and no one cares. There is no judgement. Failure is expected.

For adults, it’s exactly the opposite. We expect adults to be good at everything they do all the time. If you fall down, people are going to laugh. If you make a grammatical mistake, people are going to judge you. No wonder most adults are scared of trying new things. They don’t want to fail publicly.

So that’s the answer. To get good at failing, we need to start playing and stop taking ourselves too seriously. We need to allow ourselves to fail. Even publicly.
