The original purpose of my blog was to journal my thoughts and learnings. But I’ve recently noticed that many people I interview for potential roles, look me up and read this blog in an effort to know how to impress me. I thought I’d make it easy for them.

Firstly, be yourself and be real. Don’t try to impress me by quoting things from my blog or my LinkedIn profile. The interview is about you, not me. I’m genuinely trying to get to know you. Your knowledge of me is irrelevant towards that purpose.

Second, if I cannot understand who you are during the allocated time, I’ll walk away confused. When I’m confused, I tend to say no. So the best way to prepare for the interview is to come up with a short and accurate description of who you are as a human being. Most of us do not understand ourselves very well so it’s important to take the time to reflect. Bottomline: Make it easy for me to get to know you.

My first question would be: “Tell me your story”. Here I’m looking for a short 5 minute version of where you grew up, what you studied, what have you worked on, what are the things you are good at and what are the things you are bad at. I’m just trying to get to know you. There is no judgement. Don’t be afraid to tell me about your failures and where you need to improve. It’ll make me like you more. We’re all human after all.

Working together is like any other human relationship. We have to be compatible, we have to like each other and we have to do great things together. These are the things I’m looking for.

I love intelligence, curiosity and passion. I love people who deeply care about their work and who are proud of what they do. People who’d go the extra mile to take care of the little things even when it doesn’t always make sense just because their pride wouldn’t let them leave something imperfect. People who love to play just because they’re curious to find out how the universe works. People who are very open about their mistakes because that’s the only way to improve. People who are willing to do just about anything and no work is beneath them because there is beauty to be found in even the most mundane tasks and because there is always a higher purpose.

These are the things I’m trying to find out during our conversation. And if you’re not this person, don’t fake it. It’s not a reflection on you. It only means we’re not compatible and that’s okay. If we get this wrong, we’d both be miserable working together and what good would that do to either of us?
