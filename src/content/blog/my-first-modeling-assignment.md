This one time I was asked to be a model for an ad in a print magazine. They weren’t going to pay me but they promised to take professional pictures and share them with me. I thought that was a fair trade. Besides, I could then buy a print copy and send it to my parents to they could finally be proud of their son. Luckily, I didn’t have to buy the magazine. One day I received a package addressed to me that contained a few copies of the magazine. One of them is now proudly displayed at my parents’ house. Mission accomplished!
