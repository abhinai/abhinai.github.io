Reading this book changed my life.

I realized that setbacks and obstacles are a fact of how the world operates. They are like entropy. They are always going to happen. That is how this world works!

I’ve stopped expecting everything to go smoothly, because I’ve come to see that obstacles aren’t an interruption of the path — they are the path. For a long time, I treated every setback like a personal failure or an event of bad luck. But then, I came to see that challenges are a fundamental fact of existence, and once I started fully expecting them to appear, my entire mindset shifted.

When you stop viewing a problem as a “disaster” and start seeing it as a natural phenomenon the stress begins to evaporate. You find yourself at peace because you aren’t fighting reality anymore. Instead of being paralyzed, you simply acknowledge that this was bound to happen eventually. It removes the emotional weight and leaves you with a clear head.

The real shift happens when you move from mere acceptance to actual positivity. Since the obstacle is there anyway, we can train ourselves to find the utility in it. Every setback contains a specific opportunity — a chance to practice patience, to rethink a flawed strategy, or to develop a skill I would have otherwise ignored.
