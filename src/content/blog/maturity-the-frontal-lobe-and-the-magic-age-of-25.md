The frontal lobe is a part of the brain that is located right behind the forehead. It is responsible for speech, motor co-ordination, planning, reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making and regulating emotions.

The frontal lobe is one of the last parts of the brain to fully mature. It typically reaches full maturity in the mid-20s, around the age 25.

So 25 is the magic age that truly separates childhood from adulthood. We see examples of this all around us every day. Those of us who crossed that age remember that something changed around that time and we started feeling like adults. **I remember that sometime around that age, I became “me”.** I also remember that technical interview questions that felt impossibly hard before, suddenly became trivially easy to solve.

After hitting the age of 25, it somehow became easier for me to talk to anyone in the age ranges of 25–75 and have a meaningful conversation. I also suddenly started finding people below the age of 25 to be insufferable.

Most people below the age of 25 simply can’t be trusted with making significant life changing decisions. Their brains just aren’t there yet.

**Most countries consider 18 to be the legal age of adulthood. I think biology dictates that we should revise this number to 25.**
