I highly recommend reading the book: “Money: The True Story of a Made-Up Thing” by Jacob Goldstein. The author does a great job of explaining just how “made up” the concept of money really is.

Money is something that can be readily exchanged for other goods and services. For a long time in human history, gold was money. Gold is scarce and there is a (roughly) fixed amount of gold in the world. When paper dollars were introduced they represented actual gold held by the bank. Anybody could walk into a bank and exchange their dollars for a fixed amount of gold.

For various reasons, most countries moved away from the gold standard nearly a century ago. Today, Dollars, Pounds and Euros are backed by nothing. Absolutely nothing. No gold, no silver, no copper, nothing. These currencies are money and have value only because the respective governments say so.

Think about it. A dollar has value only because the US government says it has value. That’s it.
