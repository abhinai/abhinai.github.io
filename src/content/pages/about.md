I grew up in a small town in India, studied at IIT Delhi, and somehow ended up in Silicon Valley.

I started my career mining data at Yahoo, worked on ads at Microsoft, and had a brief stint at Facebook. Eventually, I joined Mashgin, where I decided computer vision was something I should probably learn. So I taught myself between 10 PM and 3 AM every night. Not the most sustainable study schedule, but it worked.

Today, I run Mashgin, where we're trying to make the physical world a little more intelligent.

Outside of work, I love running, long walks, and good coffee. I run a six-minute mile every day, mostly to convince myself I'm not getting older.

This blog is where I collect my thoughts, questions, and occasional half-baked theories. I write mostly to make sense of things. If someone else finds it useful, that's a bonus.
